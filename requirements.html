Admissions / Requirements

What families may need.

Everything you need to bring to your first meeting with us, in one place.

Birth certificate

From a recognized hospital

Confirms the child's official date of birth and identity.

Proof of home address

e.g. a recent utility bill

Helps us confirm the family's home address for our records.

Child's immunization card

Provides a record of vaccinations to ensure the child meets health requirements.

Most recent school report

Shows the child's academic performance and progress from their previous school, where applicable.

Four recent passport photographs

Used for identification and official school records.

Parent or guardian contact information

So we can reach you quickly about your child's day-to-day progress.
